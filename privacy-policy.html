Privacy Policy for Shooting Hours

Last updated: November 4, 2026

This policy explains what information Shooting Hours ("the app") collects, how it is used, and who it is shared with. The app is developed and published by Elizabeth Bornhoeft ("we," "us," or "our").

Information we collect

Shooting Hours is designed to work without requiring an account, tracking, or advertising. The information it handles is limited to the data you enter into the app to set your shooting dates and alarms:

  • Shooting dates and hours you add — the dates, start/end shooting hours, and alarm times you create in the app.
  • Seasons — any named groups (e.g. "Deer season") you create to organize your dates, along with their colors.
  • App preferences — settings such as your chosen vibration pattern and which alarm toggles are enabled.

We do not collect your name, contact details, precise location, device identifiers, or any analytics or usage data. The app does not use your device's location, and it contains no advertising or analytics software development kits (SDKs).

How your information is stored and synced

The data listed above is stored on your device and, if you are signed in to iCloud, synced privately through Apple's CloudKit service to your own iCloud account so it is available across your devices. This sync is handled entirely by Apple's iCloud infrastructure under Apple's own privacy terms; we do not operate any servers of our own and do not have access to this data.

Sharing dates with another person

If you choose to share dates with someone else, the app packages the dates, shooting hours, and season name/color you select into a link, then hands that link to your device's own share sheet so you can send it however you choose — for example by Messages, Mail, or AirDrop. This is a one-time transfer, not a live connection: opening the link imports those dates as the recipient's own independent copy, and nothing continues to sync between your copy and theirs afterward. The data travels only to whoever you personally choose to send it to; we never see it or have access to it.

Notifications

Shooting Hours asks for permission to send local notifications so it can alert you at the alarm times you set. These are scheduled entirely on your device; we do not send push notifications and have no server that contacts you.

Data sharing with third parties

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties. The only data transmission involved is your own private iCloud sync, handled by Apple, and — if you choose to use it — sending a one-time share link to someone you personally pick.

Your choices

  • You can delete any date, season, or your entire alarm history at any time from within the app.
  • You can stop iCloud sync for the app at any time in your device's Settings.
  • You can disable notification permissions at any time in your device's Settings.

Children's privacy

Shooting Hours is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
